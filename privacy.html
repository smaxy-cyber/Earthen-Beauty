🔒 Your Privacy Matters

Privacy Policy

How Earthen Beauty by Nupur safeguards your personal information, protects transaction data, and honors your privacy.

Effective: September 2026 Compliant with IT Act, 2000
At Earthen Beauty by Nupur, we deeply value the trust you place in us when sharing your personal information. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we collect, how it is used to deliver our artisanal products, and the robust security protocols in place to protect your data. We adhere strictly to the Information Technology Act, 2000 and associated Indian digital privacy frameworks.

1 Information We Collect

We only collect the essential information required to fulfill your orders, provide customer support, and improve your studio experience:

  • Personal Contact Information: Name, delivery address, billing address, phone/WhatsApp number, and email address provided during checkout or account registration.
  • Order & Transaction Records: Purchased item IDs, product names, quantities, selected fragrance customizations, order totals, and generated invoice numbers.
  • Communication Records: Feedback, custom bulk order requests, scent inquiries, and WhatsApp/email messages exchanged with our studio.
  • Technical & Browsing Data: Anonymized device information, browser type, IP address, and session timestamps used solely for website performance optimization and cart maintenance.

2 Zero Storage of Financial Data (Razorpay Security)

We treat payment security with the highest level of diligence:

  • All online payments on our website are processed directly by Razorpay Software Private Limited, an RBI-licensed payment aggregator maintaining strict PCI-DSS (Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard) Level 1 certification.
  • No Financial Data Stored by Us: Earthen Beauty never collects, sees, processes, or stores your credit/debit card numbers, CVV security codes, card expiration dates, netbanking passwords, or UPI PINs on our servers or databases.
  • All financial transaction data is encrypted end-to-end using industry-standard 256-bit TLS/SSL encryption directly between your browser and Razorpay's banking infrastructure.
PCI-DSS Certified Security:

Your payment credentials never touch our web servers. They are tokenized and processed exclusively through Razorpay's RBI-regulated financial gateway.

3 How We Use Your Information

We utilize your personal information solely for legitimate e-commerce operations:

  • Order Fulfillment & Delivery: Sharing your name, delivery address, and contact number with authorized courier aggregators (primarily Shiprocket and verified Indian shipping partners like Blue Dart, Delhivery, DTDC) to deliver your fragile handcrafted packages safely.
  • Order Notifications: Sending automated email or SMS notifications for order confirmations, dispatch tracking numbers, and delivery status updates.
  • Customer Care: Resolving order inquiries, transit damage reports, and bulk gifting requests.
  • Scent Club Newsletter (Optional): Sending seasonal batch reveals, candle care rituals, and promotional offers only if you explicitly choose to subscribe. You may unsubscribe at any time with one click.

4 No Sale or Unauthorized Sharing of Personal Data

We respect your privacy as if it were our own. We do not sell, rent, lease, trade, or monetize your personal information with any third-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing networks. Information is disclosed strictly to trusted service providers (payment gateway and shipping couriers) essential to completing your order, or when required by court order or law enforcement authorities under Indian law.

5 Cookies & Local Storage

Our website uses minimal, functional cookies and browser local storage to enhance your shopping experience:

  • To remember items added to your shopping cart as you navigate between pages.
  • To remember your login status if you choose to create a customer account.
  • We do not employ intrusive cross-site tracking cookies. You can modify your browser settings to disable cookies, though this may prevent the shopping cart from retaining your selected items.

6 Data Retention & Security Protocols

We implement technical and organizational security measures—including SSL certificates, sanitized input handling, parameterized database queries, and restricted admin portal authentication—to safeguard your information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. Customer order records are retained for statutory accounting and tax compliance purposes as mandated by Indian GST and commercial regulations.

7 Your Data Rights

As a valued customer, you have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal contact information we hold regarding your account.
  • Request correction or updating of any inaccurate address or contact details.
  • Request deletion of your registered customer account (subject to statutory order and tax record retention requirements).
  • Opt-out of any promotional email communications at any time.
